## Description (~3,900 characters)

**SpellFlare** turns spelling practice into an adventure your kids will actually want to play. Designed for Grades 1-7, SpellFlare speaks words aloud and lets children spell them using voice recognition or a kid-friendly custom keyboard — no autocorrect, no shortcuts, just real spelling practice.

**HOW IT WORKS**
1. Listen — Hear the word spoken clearly, plus example sentences for context
2. Spell — Say the letters out loud or type them on the custom keyboard
3. Celebrate — Earn coins, unlock achievements, and watch confetti fly!

**50 LEVELS PER GRADE**
Words are carefully curated for each grade level, from simple sight words in Grade 1 to spelling bee competition words in Grade 7. Every child learns at their own pace with no time pressure.

**COINS, SHOP & REWARDS**
Earn coins for every level — more coins for fewer mistakes! Spend them on fun bee skins, background themes, and celebration effects. Daily check-ins award bonus coins and build streaks.

**30+ ACHIEVEMENTS**
From "Buzzing Beginner" to "Master of the Hive," kids earn achievements across skill, streaks, and grade completion categories in Common, Rare, Epic, and Legendary rarities — with Game Center support.

**FAMILY-FRIENDLY PROFILES**
Up to 4 player profiles on one device. Each child gets their own progress, coins, achievements, and avatar. Siblings share the app — not their scores.

**iPHONE & APPLE WATCH**
Practice on iPhone at home or Apple Watch on the go. Progress syncs automatically between devices.

**BUILT FOR KIDS, TRUSTED BY PARENTS**
- No ads during gameplay — ever
- No accounts, logins, or data collection
- No external links or social media
- Parent gate before any purchase
- All data stays on your device
